MathRelaxing Atelier Resume Kit — How to use
============================================

1. Start with a SAMPLE file to see finished spacing and tone.
2. Open the matching blank TEMPLATE and replace placeholders.
3. Choose a system:
   · Atelier — centered, letterspaced name, double gold rules (most elegant)
   · Gallery — left-aligned, modern, gold top bar
   · ATS — use for online portals that strip design
4. Cover letter + references match the Atelier header language.
5. Export PDF from Word (File → Save As → PDF) for email applications.

Tips
· Keep one page for most industry roles; two only if senior with deep research.
· Prefer metrics over adjectives.
· Do not convert Atelier/Gallery through aggressive ATS parsers — use the ATS file.
