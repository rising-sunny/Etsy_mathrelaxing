DESCRIPCION  (pegar tal cual)
==============================================================================
Which offer is really the better deal? Students compare unit prices, check quantities, and make shopping decisions they can back up with the maths.

This printable goes past picking the lowest sticker price. Students also weigh whether a purchase actually meets their needs and fits the budget — including one problem where the lowest price per item is NOT the cheapest way to buy.

► What's included (15 pages)
• 12 offer comparisons across two levels — 6 whole-dollar, 6 decimal
• A worked example that separates unit value from total spending
• A reusable comparison organizer
• A club supply challenge with a $20 budget
• A three-question exit ticket
• Worked answer keys, teacher guidance and suggested scoring

► Skills
Calculate and compare unit rates; label dollars per item; compare prices per 100 g and per 100 mL; recognise equal unit prices; and choose whole-pack purchases within a budget.

► In the classroom or at home
Built for grades 6–7. Works for independent or partner practice, a unit-rate review, a sub plan, or homeschool. Level 1 uses whole-dollar rates, Level 2 introduces decimals, and the two levels use different problems — so you can assign both.

► Format
15-page PDF at US Letter: cover, teacher guide, 8 student-facing pages, 4 answer-key pages and terms of use. Black-and-white friendly. Not editable. No devices and no cutting required.

All stores and prices are fictional. Items within each comparison are assumed to be the same quality.
