# Letter of Recommendation — competitive research + kit plan

**eRank (now):** 1,160 searches in Jun 2026 · avg 1,010 · mom **1.15** · **KD 1** · **605 comp**

## SERP findings (Etsy API, 2026-07-17)

| Pattern | What winners do |
|---------|-----------------|
| Price | Real digital: **$1.50–$6.65** for 1–2 files; kit can sit **$6.99–8.99** |
| Formats | **Word** always · often Google Docs · sometimes Canva · PDF sample |
| Hearts | Tops ~80–100; many &lt;20 → market not owned by mega-sellers |
| Verticals | Job/employee · academic/grad · teacher/college · character/personal · court · nurse · landlord · sorority |
| Copy | “Editable”, “instant download”, “sample included”, “Google Docs + Word” |
| Design | Mostly plain black Times / minimal Canva — **little editorial craft** |

### High-fav examples (signal, not copy)

- Professional LOR Word+Docs — ~$2.66–6.65 · 70–90 favs · template + sometimes sample  
- Character / court reference — high favs; legal vertical separate (we skip court to avoid liability tone)  
- Job referral bundles — $4.50–5.00 · sample + template  

### Gaps we fill (MathRelaxing)

1. **Multi-situation pack** (not one generic letter)  
2. **Editorial letterhead** (navy/gold/cream) vs plain docs  
3. **Filled STEM-quality samples** (metrics, specific praise)  
4. **How-to guide** (structure, phrases, length, what to avoid)  
5. Cross-sell with **Atelier / Editorial resume kits**
