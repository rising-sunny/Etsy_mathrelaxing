### Design conventions (SERP)

1. **Single column** for ATS (no multi-col tables that break parsers)
2. **Section bars** or hairlines under SUMMARY / EXPERIENCE / EDUCATION
3. **Centered name** + contact line
4. **Bullet metrics** (`+12%`, `$4.2M`)
5. **Mockups:** white paper on desk, gold pens, marble, **“ATS friendly” badge**
6. **Always include** cover letter + references in winners
7. **Formats promised:** Word · Google Docs · Pages (docx import covers all three)
