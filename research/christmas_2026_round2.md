## Recommendations (in order)
1. **Geometric 3D Paper Ornaments** (digital, ~$6.99): printable nets for polyhedra
   (Platonic solids, stellated dodecahedron, Moravian/Froebel-style stars), with fold/glue
   guides, PDF + SVG for Cricut. Supply is tiny for "moravian star / dodecahedron /
   polyhedron template"; the nets are pure math, generated exactly.
2. **Printable Math Christmas Cards** (digital, ~$5.50): 8-10 folding cards with math puns
   and our generative art (fractal tree, golden spiral star, Koch snowflake).
3. **Fractal / Math ceramic ornaments** (Printify POD, ~$19.99): Koch snowflake, Mandelbrot
   star, golden-spiral bauble; gift for math lovers; low competition for "fractal ornament".
4. Quick spin-off: **Christmas Sudoku pack** ($3.99) from the logic book's 30 sudoku.
