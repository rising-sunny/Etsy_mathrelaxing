Notes
- "christmas math escape room" has only 35 listings and almost no demand: sell the escape
  room as a *Christmas Escape Room for Kids* with math/logic inside, not as "math".
- Web: printable game bundles are the volume leaders (bingo 11k+ sales, 90-game bundle
  6k+); kids' printable escape rooms (8-13) and I Spy are top sellers.
- Timing: digital Christmas demand peaks late Nov → ~20 Dec; list by end of October.

Recommended builds
1. Christmas Escape Room for Kids (8-13): story + ~8 logic/math/code puzzles + answer key.
2. Christmas STEM Challenge Cards: build/engineering challenges (gingerbread, candy canes,
   sleigh ramp, ornament parachute) for classroom/homeschool.
3. Christmas Brain Teasers & Logic Puzzle book (teens/adults): logic grids, sudoku,
   cryptograms, nonograms — high price point.
